### Highlights ✨

- Introduce the public `update_targets` action (`vizro.actions.update_targets`) to refresh a page's figures and dynamic filters on demand, for example to apply controls together on a `Button` click. See the [user guide on applying controls with a button](https://vizro.readthedocs.io/en/stable/pages/user-guides/controls/#apply-controls-with-a-button) for more details. ([#1538](https://github.com/mckinsey/vizro/pull/1538))
