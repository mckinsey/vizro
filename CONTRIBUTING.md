# Contributing

Contributions of all experience levels are welcome! If you're interested in making a contribution, please refer to our [contributing guide](https://vizro.readthedocs.io/en/stable/pages/explanation/contributing/) for more information.

**Note**: We require all contributors to understand and take full responsibility for every change they submit. Pull requests containing AI-generated code that the contributor has not personally understood, tested, and verified will typically be closed without review.

We do not welcome AI contributions by bots with no human in the loop. To request permission to submit a PR, the human owner of the bot must open an issue for approval in advance of the PR's submission.
